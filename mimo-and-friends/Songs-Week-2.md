## 🦷 Brush Your Teeth with Mimo

**Style:**
```
gentle upbeat children's song, ukulele, glockenspiel, soft claps, warm friendly female vocal, very clear English, tempo 100 bpm
```

**Lyrics:**
```
[Intro]
Hello friends, it's Mimo time!
Let's brush our teeth! Let's go, friends!

[Verse 1]
Get your toothbrush, get your paste
Just a tiny bit, don't waste!
Up and down and round and round
Brushing makes a happy sound!

[Chorus]
Brush, brush, brush your teeth
Top and bottom, under and beneath!
Brush, brush, nice and clean
The shiniest smile you've ever seen!

[Verse 2]
Brush the front, now brush the back
Brush the sides, don't miss a crack!
Count with me, one, two, three
Sparkly teeth for you and me!

[Chorus]
Brush, brush, brush your teeth
Top and bottom, under and beneath!
Brush, brush, nice and clean
The shiniest smile you've ever seen!

[Bridge]
Swish, swish, swish the water
Spit it out, just like you oughta!

[Final Chorus]
Brush, brush, morning and night
Mimo's teeth are shiny bright!
Brush, brush, big smile, ding!
Now let's all smile and sing!

[Outro]
Bye-bye friends, see you next time!
```

**المشاهد:** Mimo قدام المراية، وبيدهن معجون صغير، وبيفرّش فوق وتحت، وبيعد على صوابعه، وبيمضمض، وفي الآخر بيضحك وسنانه بتلمع ✨.

## 🔢 Counting 1 to 10 with Mimo

**Style:**
```
bouncy children's counting song, xylophone, marimba, hand claps, cheerful female vocal with kids choir, very clear English, tempo 98 bpm
```

**Lyrics:**
```
[Intro]
Hello friends, it's Mimo time!
Let's count together! Ready?

[Verse 1]
One little banana, yummy and yellow
Two little ducks say quack, hello!
Three little stars up in the sky
Four little birds go flying by!

[Chorus]
Count with me, count with me
One, two, three, it's easy, see?
Hold up your fingers, let's have fun
Counting, counting, everyone!

[Verse 2]
Five little fish go splash, splash, splash
Six little cars go dash, dash, dash!
Seven little apples on the tree
Eight little bees go buzz with me!

[Verse 3]
Nine little balloons float up high
Ten little friends wave bye-bye!

[Chorus]

One, two, three, it's easy, see?
```

## 🧼 Wash Your Hands Song

**Style:**
```
playful children's song, acoustic guitar, bubbly sound effects, hand claps, friendly male vocal, very clear English, tempo 104 bpm
```

**Lyrics:**
```
[Intro]
Hello friends, it's Mimo time!
Oops, dirty hands! Let's wash them!

[Verse 1]
Turn on the water, splash, splash, splash
Get some soap, quick as a flash!
Rub your palms, then rub the backs
Bubbles, bubbles, stacks and stacks!

[Chorus]
Wash, wash, wash your hands
Bubbles everywhere, oh what fun!
Wash, wash, scrub, scrub, scrub
Clean, clean hands for everyone!

[Verse 2]
In between your fingers, too
Your thumbs, your nails, that's what we do!
Rinse them off and shake, shake, shake
Dry them with a towel, no mistake!

[Chorus]
Wash, wash, wash your hands
Bubbles everywhere, oh what fun!
Wash, wash, scrub, scrub, scrub
Clean, clean hands for everyone!

[Bridge]
Before we eat and after play
We wash our hands every day!

[Outro]
Squeaky clean! Bye-bye friends, see you next time!
```

**المشاهد:** إيدين Mimo عليها طين بعد ما لعب مع Pip، وبعدين يفتح الحنفية، وتطلع فقاعات صابون كبيرة بتلمع، ويفرك بين الصوابع، ويغسل، وينشّف بالفوطة، وفي الآخر يرفع إيديه وهي نضيفة وبتلمع ✨.
